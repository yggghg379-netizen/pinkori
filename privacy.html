Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 • Domain: pinkori-id.vercel.app

1 Information We Collect

To provide account services, the pinkori-id.vercel.app service stores only the minimum data required:

  • Your username and encrypted (hashed) password;
  • Your unique Discord ID and username, if you voluntarily link your profile through Discord OAuth2.

We do not request your email address, avatar URLs, Discord password, private messages, or server list.

2 How We Use Your Data

  • To create your account and sign in to the Pinkori launcher and games;
  • To link your Discord profile and help protect your account.

3 Data Storage and Deletion

  • Passwords are stored only in encrypted (hashed) form.
  • We do not share or sell your data to third parties.
  • You can unlink Discord at any time or request account deletion from the project administrators.
