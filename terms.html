Terms of Use and Game Rules

Last updated: November 26, 2026 • Domain: pinkori-id.vercel.app

1 General Provisions

The Pinkori ID service (pinkori-id.vercel.app) provides a unified system for registration, sign-in, and account management across games and services in the Pinkori project. By creating an account, linking Discord, or launching a game, you agree to these rules.

2 Accounts and Discord Linking

  • Users are responsible for the security of their accounts and the confidentiality of their sign-in credentials.
  • Transferring, selling, or trading accounts between players is strictly prohibited.
  • Linking a Discord profile through OAuth2 is optional and is used for faster sign-in and avatar/username synchronization. You can unlink your account at any time in your profile settings or through Discord.

3 Game and Community Conduct

The following activities are prohibited in Pinkori games, the launcher, and on its servers:

  • Using offensive, obscene, or provocative usernames, avatars, or statuses.
  • Impersonating project developers, moderators, or administrators.
  • Harassing other players, provoking conflicts, spamming chat, or advertising third-party projects without approval.
  • Sharing malicious links, other players' personal information (doxxing), or prohibited content.

4 Fair Play and Technical Restrictions

  • Using cheats, injectors, third-party software, auto-clickers, exploits, or bugs to gain an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited.
  • Attempts to hack, launch DDoS attacks, create registration spam, or interfere with the servers or API of pinkori-id.vercel.app are prohibited.
  • If a player discovers a vulnerability or bug, they must report it to the administrators rather than exploit it for personal gain.

5 In-Game Economy and Items

  • In-game currency, items, and progress are part of the gameplay and have no real-world monetary value.
  • Players are prohibited from buying or selling in-game items or currency for real money.
  • The administrators reserve the right to adjust game balance, update mechanics, or reset the in-game economy when necessary for the development of the project.

6 Violations and Penalties

  • For rule violations, the administrators may issue a warning, restrict chat access, reset game progress, or suspend an account temporarily or permanently.
  • The service and games are provided "as is." The administrators are not responsible for temporary technical failures or loss of access caused by the user.
